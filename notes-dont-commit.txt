Formula breakdown:
d = measured distance from ping (3D diagonal distance from host to beacon)
z_host = host depth (from pressure sensor, e.g., 10 m below surface)
z_beacon = beacon depth (surface beacons: 0 m, or known if submerged)
Example:
Beacon on surface: z_beacon = 0
Host at 10 m depth: z_host = 10
Measured distance: d = 15 m (diagonal)
Calculate 2D distance:
2D_distance = sqrt(15² - (10 - 0)²)            = sqrt(225 - 100)            = sqrt(125)            = 11.18 m
This is the horizontal distance (radius of the circle in the x-y plane at depth 10 m).
In your case:
Surface beacons: z_beacon = 0
Host depth: z_host from pressure sensor
Use the formula to get the horizontal circle radius for each beacon
